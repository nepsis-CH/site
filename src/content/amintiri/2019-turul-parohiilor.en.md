This is the story of Nepsis Switzerland's activities in 2019, which had the Parish Tour at its heart. That is, each of us set out from the city where we lived in order to visit one parish — we took part in the Sunday service, and afterwards we talked (and sometimes also went for a walk) with the members of that parish.

Below you can see a video/collage retrospective (from [our YouTube channel](http://youtube.com/c/nepsiselvetia)), as well as the calendar of events:
