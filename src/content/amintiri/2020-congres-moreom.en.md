Three beautiful days **(25–27 September 2020)** with participants from 7 countries: now that's an adventure, especially in pandemic times.

The trip to the Alps, the guided tour of Bern, interesting talks and workshops, delicious meals and the hierarchical Divine Liturgy — have we made you curious?

You can see photos, the recordings of the talks and testimonies on the [page dedicated to the congress](/congres2020/index.html) — the Memories section.
