The patron saint of Nepsis Switzerland is [Saint John Cassian](/en/ocrotitorul/), whose feast day is 29 February.

On **29 February 2020** we all gathered in Zürich, where we had the joy of having among us His Eminence Joseph, our Metropolitan.

You can read a fuller account of how the day unfolded in [this document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TU0GYTKqo6gbw0Cs45iqSVlkhX13YTvbbMqHe-WNQHc/edit?usp=sharing).
