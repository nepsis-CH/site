Between **13 April and 26 May**, about ten of us Swiss Nepsis members rehearsed together, six times a week, the hymns of the Canon of the Resurrection. Since we lived in different cities, we rehearsed using the Zoom app.

Then, **starting on 2 June and continuing throughout 2020**, the course "The tones of Byzantine chant", held by Nepsis Switzerland, was addressed to people everywhere. You can read the testimonies of those from various countries who took part in this course during 2020 on the [Byzantine Chant Group](/en/psaltica/) page.
