The patron saint of Nepsis Switzerland is [Saint John Cassian](/en/ocrotitorul/), whose feast day is 29 February.

On **27 February 2021** we all gathered in Bern, where we had the joy of having among us His Grace Marc, who gave a talk on the theme "The will of man and the will of God", followed by a Mărțișor fair. The mărțișoare had been made by the Swiss Nepsis members.
