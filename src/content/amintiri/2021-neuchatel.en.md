At the invitation of Father Marius, we arrived on Saturday, **12 June 2021**. We prayed the Akathist of St. Nectarios together, after which the warm-hearted members of the Neuchâtel parish sat us down to a meal. Then came an open discussion with His Grace Marc, who once again gladdened us with his presence. At the end, we set off on a hike to the Gorges de l'Areuse, where we immortalised ourselves on this bridge with the help of a drone.
