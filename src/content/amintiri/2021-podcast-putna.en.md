We gathered virtually on Zoom to listen to the accounts of the 4 Nepsis Switzerland members who took part in the Putna Celebration 2021 — The Continuity of an Ideal, which was held at Putna Monastery (Suceava County) from 14 to 16 August 2021.

We relived the events, we watched video clips and photos accompanied by the commentary of those who were there.
