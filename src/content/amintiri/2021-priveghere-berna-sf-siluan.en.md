On 23 September 2021 we gathered in Bern, one year after the NEPSIS MOREOM 2020 Congress held in this parish. Fathers Laurențiu and Cătălin served the vigil dedicated to Saint Silouan the Athonite. We had the joy of having among us a neptic from Nepsis Ireland 🍀 as well, who sang with us in the choir. Praise be to God for all things 🙏.
