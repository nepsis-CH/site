11 September — a group of neptic cyclists gathered in the Neuchâtel area where, led by Father Marius Manea (the parish priest of the church in Neuchâtel), we rode a 50-kilometre route on our bikes along the lakeshore, past the vineyards and the medieval castles of the region.
