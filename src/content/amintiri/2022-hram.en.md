On Saturday, 26 February 2022, we gathered in Lausanne to celebrate the patron of Nepsis Switzerland, [Saint John Cassian](/en/ocrotitorul/). His Eminence Iosif, Metropolitan of Western and Southern Europe, was among us and answered our questions, together with Father Adrian and Father Mihai.
