At the beginning of December 2023, we co-organised three screenings of the documentary film "Where Are You, Adam?", about the life of the monks of Mount Athos.

On Saturday, 2 December, the film was screened in collaboration with the Romanian Saints Parish in Lausanne (in the morning) and the Saint John the Baptist Parish in Geneva (in the evening).

On Tuesday, 5 December, the film was screened in collaboration with the Greek association Melissa in Lausanne, in the evening.
