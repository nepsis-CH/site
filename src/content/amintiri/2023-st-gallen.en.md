On Saturday, 28 October 2023, the Lord gave us the gift of a sunny day and a guide passionate about the details of the abbey in St. Gallen: we visited the library and the permanent and temporary exhibitions in the cellars of the complex.

Together with Father Mihail we then went to the Romanian church in St. Gallen, where he read the Akathist of All Saints, and the participants from various parishes got to know one another better over a small agape meal.

There is still a lot left to visit, especially the cathedral and the relics of the saints it shelters. We hope to repeat the experience, with the Lord's help.
