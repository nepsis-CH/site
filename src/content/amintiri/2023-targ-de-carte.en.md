In the parish of Lausanne we organised, with the blessing of Father Adrian, a book fair that began in June and carried on through July and August.

The money raised at this fair will be used to pay for the translation of the service of Saint John Cassian (the patron saint of Nepsis CH) and to fund the next Nepsis CH activities.
