In 2024 we celebrated the feast of our patron saint, Saint John Cassian, on Saturday 24 February at the Romanian Orthodox Church in Baden.

On this occasion, Father Adrian Anca, who has been organising the online Byzantine chant courses for years, was ordained a spiritual father by His Grace Nectarie of Brittany. We wish Father Adrian much health and zeal in the mission he has received!
