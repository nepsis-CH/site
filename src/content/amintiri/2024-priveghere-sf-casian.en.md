We took advantage of the fact that 2024 is a leap year and organised a vigil service on Wednesday evening, 28 February, in honour of Saint John Cassian.

Since it fell in the middle of the week, two vigils were held, in Basel and in Lausanne, so that as many people as possible could take part.
