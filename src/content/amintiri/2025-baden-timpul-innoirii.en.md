On Sunday, 5 October, with the help of the Lord and of Saint John Cassian, we managed to bring together in one place young people from different Swiss cantons, as part of the project “Nepsis Switzerland: A Time of Renewal”.

We took part in the Divine Liturgy at the Romanian Parish in Baden, after which we had lunch together.

The day continued with Nepsis presentation sessions, team building and brainstorming, in which we shared ideas, plans and enthusiasm for the future.

Both the new and the long-standing Nepsis volunteers came with plenty of energy and wonderful proposals for the next activities — we hope to put them all into practice!

It was a genuine joy to get to know one another (better) and to talk about the future of Nepsis!

Thanks be to the Lord, thank you to the Baden parish for its hospitality and organisation, and to everyone who took part in this blessed beginning!
