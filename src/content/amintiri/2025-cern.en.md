25 young Nepsis members eager to learn about the research at CERN that has led to scientific discoveries used in all sorts of fields, such as HTML and the WWW!

Some of the participants then went on to visit the Coptic church in Geneva, to venerate the relics of Saints Verena and Maurice (saints who fell asleep in the Lord in the 3rd–4th centuries, on the territory of present-day Switzerland).
