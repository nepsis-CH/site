God help us 🙏! On 1 August we managed to meet up with Father Dionysius and to organise the hike with catechesis in the Lauterbrunnen area.

📖 The catechesis was about the essence of Holy Communion, with the emphasis on our own co-working in this mystery. Among the references used during the catechesis we mention the Gospel of Matthew, ch. 26, and Origen — Commentary on the Gospel of Matthew, ch. 26.

🚶‍♀ We went for an easy walk through the Lauterbrunnen valley, with stops at the waterfalls: Staubbach, Spissbach, Aegertenbach and at the underground ⛰️ Trümmelbach falls.

A few moments are captured in the photos alongside.
