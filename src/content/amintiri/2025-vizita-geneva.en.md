Dear Neptics, God help us!

On 2 November, the church in Geneva had the blessing of receiving His Eminence Metropolitan Joseph on a pastoral visit. His visit brought light, peace and joy into the hearts of the faithful, and the hierarchical service celebrated on this occasion was a moment of deep communion and spiritual emotion.

In his word of teaching, His Eminence urged the faithful to live the faith with an open heart and to show their love through the works of mercy, recalling the parable of the merciless rich man.

After the service, the young people of Nepsis and the other young people of the community had an open dialogue with His Eminence, receiving answers full of wisdom, gentleness and fatherly love.

Through the care of Father Adrian Diaconu and the involvement of the parishioners, the whole day unfolded in an atmosphere of celebration, joy and prayer, discovering the beauty of communion in Christ.
