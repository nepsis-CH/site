God help us!

Today we really enjoyed a gorgeous autumn day and a proper hike near the Pilatus summit, close to Lucerne!

We started the day with the traveller’s prayer and plenty of energy. After a short walk at the foot of Lake Lucerne, the trail took us into the hilly area, where we climbed around 450 metres.

The spiritual conversations and the themed notes prepared by Simona drew us into many long discussions along the way, so much so that we even forgot to have lunch 🫣.

We finished the trail and, after regaining our strength over dinner, we set off for the parish in Lucerne, where we celebrated Vespers.

The responses at the chanter’s stand were given by Luca and Dragomir.

Glory to God for such a beautiful day, and thank you for taking part.

The Nepsis Switzerland team
