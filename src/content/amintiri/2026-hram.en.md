On 28 February 2026 we celebrated the patron saint feast of Nepsis Switzerland, Saint John Cassian, at the “Protection of the Mother of God” Monastery in Les Sciernes d’Albeuve, with His Grace Teofil of Iberia as our guest.

The programme included Matins, the Hierarchical Divine Liturgy, lunch at the monastery and a conversation with His Grace Teofil about the writings of Saint John Cassian.

You can listen to His Grace Teofil’s sermon on our YouTube channel.
