The Nepsis patron saint feast of “Saint John Cassian” takes place at the Romanian monastery in Switzerland, where our guest will be His Grace Theophilos of Iberia.

📍 **Location:** Monastery “Protection of the Mother of God”, Route de la Gare 39, 1669 Les Sciernes d'Albeuve — [map](https://share.google/kWnAeQKGc7boBN2dC)

⏰ **Programme:**

- 7:30 – Matins
- 9:00 – Hierarchical Divine Liturgy
- Lunch at the monastery
- Dialogue with Bishop Theophilos: the writings of St John Cassian

🏠 **Accommodation:** for those who wish, it is possible to stay at the guesthouse in the village on Friday night or Saturday night (or both). The options are:

➡️ 4 rooms with private bathroom (2 beds):

- 1 person – 70 CHF
- 2 people – 100 CHF

➡️ 4 rooms with shared bathroom:

- 1 person – 55 CHF
- 2 people – 80 CHF
- 3 people – 115 CHF
- 4 people – 150 CHF

Bookings are made individually, following the instructions on the website: [lapintedelys.ch](https://lapintedelys.ch/language/en/). If you mention that you are part of the monastery group, you receive a 10% discount 💸.
