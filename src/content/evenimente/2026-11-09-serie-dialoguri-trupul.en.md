Nepsis Switzerland is joining the series of dialogues “The Body” with Father Marc-Antoine Costa de Beauregard, beginning on Monday 9 November 2026.

At the invitation and with the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan Joseph, Nepsis Switzerland joins the young people of Nepsis France for this series of talks.

It is a path of monthly meetings on the body and on understanding the human person in the light of the Orthodox faith. The meetings are held in French and in Romanian.

**First meeting: Monday 9 November 2026. Topic: “The dimensions of the human person”.**

The first discussion will lay the ground for a shared understanding. In the meetings that follow we will take up more specific questions about the body, freedom, sexuality, the relationship with others and self-love.

The meetings take place at the Orthodox parish of Saint-Germain-et-saint-Cloud (21, rue de Montbuisson, 78430 Louveciennes). For those in Switzerland, you take part online, on Zoom.

⚠ Who can take part: members of Nepsis Switzerland who live in Switzerland and are between 14 and 35 years old.

The time and the Zoom link will be sent shortly before the talk. If you would like to take part, write to us at <a href="#" class="email-protejat" data-x="nepsis.ch" data-y="mitropolia" data-z="eu"><span class="sr-only">E-mail</span></a> with your name, your age and your parish. You can send us your questions at the same address.

We encourage young people to come with their questions and to follow the whole path, because each discussion prepares the ones that follow.

We look forward to seeing you,  
The Nepsis Switzerland team
