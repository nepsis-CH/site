---
title: "About Nepsis Switzerland"
description: "Who we are: the fellowship of young Romanian Orthodox Christians in Switzerland."
---

Nepsis is an organisation of the young people of the Romanian Orthodox Metropolis of Western and Southern Europe, which seeks to offer a setting where young people can get to know one another and join forces to keep the flame of faith and tradition burning in the countries where they live.

With the blessing of His Eminence Father Iosif (Metropolitan of Western and Southern Europe of the Romanian Orthodox Church), the Nepsis Switzerland branch took wing at the end of 2018 and has as its patron saint [**Saint John Cassian of Dobruja**](/en/ocrotitorul/), the founder of Western monasticism (4th century), celebrated by the Romanian Orthodox Church on 29 February.
