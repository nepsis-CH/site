Nepsis Switzerland is the fellowship of young Romanian Orthodox Christians in Switzerland, under the omophorion of the Romanian Orthodox Metropolis of Western and Southern Europe. We are here for young people between 14 and 35. No formality is needed to get to know us: the door is open to anyone who wants to live their faith together with others.

## First step - come to a Nepsis Switzerland gathering!

The simplest way into the fellowship is to come to what we do: pilgrimages, hikes with catechesis, vigils, visits to parishes and monasteries, discussion evenings. All of them are open to everyone.

Have a look at the [events page](/en/evenimente/) to see what is coming up, or at the [memories page](/en/amintiri/) to see what our gatherings have been like so far.

- [Subscribe to the newsletter](/en/newsletter/) to receive the important news by e-mail.
- Follow us on social media; the links are at the bottom of the page.

## Get involved

Want to do more than just come along? We need volunteers! You can help us organise the next events and join the planning meetings. Tell us what you would enjoy doing; there is room for anyone who wants to pitch in.
