Nepsis Switzerland is the fellowship of young Romanian Orthodox Christians in Switzerland, under the omophorion of the Romanian Orthodox Metropolis of Western and Southern Europe. No formality is needed to get to know us: the door is open to every young person who wants to live their faith together with others.

## First step: come to a gathering

The most natural way to become part of the fellowship is to take part in our activities — pilgrimages, hikes with catechesis, vigils, visits to parishes and monasteries, discussion evenings. All of them are open to everyone.

Have a look at the [events page](/en/evenimente/) to see what is coming up, or at the [memories page](/en/amintiri/) to see what our gatherings have been like so far.

## Keep in touch with us

- **Write us an e-mail** at <a href="#" class="email-protejat" data-x="nepsis.ch" data-y="mitropolia" data-z="eu"><span class="sr-only">E-mail</span></a> and tell us a few words about yourself — we will add you to our internal **Telegram and WhatsApp** groups, where all activities are announced.
- [Subscribe to the newsletter](/en/newsletter/) to receive the important news by e-mail.
- Follow us on social media — the links are at the bottom of the page.

## Get involved

If you want to do more than take part — help organise events, sing at the kliros in the [Byzantine chant group](/en/psaltica/) or volunteer — let us know. There is always room, and everyone is needed.
